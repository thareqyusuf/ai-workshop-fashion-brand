```

Last year: bought 4,000. Sold 3,400 at full price; 600 marked down by 40%.
Forecast for this year (last year plus 10%): 3,700 units.
Stock in hand: 150 units.
Supplier: minimum order 1,500. Unit cost £15.20 at 3,000, £14.00 at 4,000,
£13.20 at 5,000. Lead time 14 weeks.
Budget for this style: £70,000.
Unsold stock: up to 300 units clear at 40% off. The rest is
written off.
Objective: maximise expected profit for this style this season.

Wear data from a wardrobe app:
Owners of this jumper logged 39.1 wears per year.
Knitwear average in the app: 25 wears per year.
In this brand's history, a style worn 10% more than the knitwear average
has sold about 2% more the following season.
Sample: 600 app users. Users added on average 4 of their 10 knitwear items to the app, mostly
ones they described as favourites.

## Workshop panel

Play the role you are given. Make Decision 1 from the sales panel. Only then turn to the wear data and make Decision 2.

Roles and private goals:

- **Merchandiser**: Hit the sales plan, keep closing stock low
- **Buyer**: Protect newness and full-price sell-through on the hero piece
- **Supplier / sourcing lead**: Keep volume above price tiers and minimums, fill capacity
- **Head of Merchandising (sign-off)**: Margin, markdown budget
- **Sustainability lead**: Reduce unsold units the brand has to report under EU rules

### Sales panel (Decision 1)

```
                         Line A: Merino Wool Crewneck   Line B: Cable Knit Cardigan
                         (carryover, 4th season)        (on trend, 2nd season)
Bought last year         6,000                          3,000
Sold at full price       78%                            55%
Sold on a markdown       18%                            30%
Left unsold              4% (240 units)                 15% (450 units)
Proposed buy this year   6,500                          3,000
Unit cost                £14.00 under 5,000 units;      £18.00
                         £12.60 at 5,000+
Supplier minimum         1,000 per colour               1,500 per colour, 2 colours planned
```

Decision 1. Set the buy for Line A and Line B. Give your confidence from 1 (low) to 5 (high) and your reason in one sentence.

```
